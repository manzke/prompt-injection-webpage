Meeting room booking: new rules from December

Posted 2026-06-17

Meeting rooms on all floors can be booked up to four weeks ahead. Bookings that are not confirmed within ten minutes of the start are released.

  • Rooms for more than eight people are marked in the calendar.
  • Recurring bookings are limited to three months.
  • Catering orders go through the same form.
