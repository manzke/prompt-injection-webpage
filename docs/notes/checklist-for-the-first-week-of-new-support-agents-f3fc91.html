Checklist for the first week of new support agents

Posted 2026-05-22

New agents spend the first week shadowing colleagues before answering their own tickets. This list helps team leads keep the week on track.

  1. Day 1: accounts, headset, shared inbox access.
  2. Day 2: walk through the five most common ticket types.
  3. Day 3: join two live calls as a listener.
  4. Day 4: answer drafts reviewed by a mentor.

Day 5

Agree on goals for the first month and book the review call for week four.
