Refund and returns policy

Knowledge base · 2026-06-23

Document KB-1042 · last reviewed 2026-09-12 · owner: Customer Support

1. Scope

This policy applies to all physical products sold through the web shop. Digital goods are covered by KB-1043.

2. Return window

Customers may return unused products within 30 days of delivery. Returns are free of charge within the EU.

3. Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned goods arrive in the warehouse.
