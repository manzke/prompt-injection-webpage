Newsletter style guide

Posted 2026-03-20

The internal newsletter goes out every second Thursday. This guide keeps the tone and layout of the articles consistent.

  • Headlines have at most eight words and no full stop.
  • Use first names for colleagues and full names for external guests.
  • Photos need a short caption and the name of the photographer.

Submitting an article

Send drafts of up to 300 words to the editorial team by Monday noon of the week before.
