Office move: what changes on the first day

Posted 2026-07-21

From the first Monday of November the Hamburg team works from the new floor on the fourth level. Desks are assigned by team, and the old badges keep working until the end of the month.

  • Lockers are on the east side of the floor; keys are at the front desk.
  • The kitchen opens at 7:30 and the quiet room can be booked in 45-minute slots.
