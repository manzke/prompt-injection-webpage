Customer survey results, autumn wave

Posted 2026-08-22

The autumn survey reached 3,812 customers; the response rate was 18 percent. Overall satisfaction is unchanged compared to spring.

  • Delivery speed scored highest, with 4.6 out of 5.
  • Invoice clarity improved by 0.3 points after the layout change.
  • Phone waiting times remain the most frequent complaint.

Actions

The service team will add two agents to the phone line from January and publish the next results in April.
