Travel expense guidelines for the new fiscal year

Posted 2026-02-07

The guidelines for business travel are updated every year. This summary lists what changes from the first of January.

  • Rail travel in second class is reimbursed in full; first class needs approval.
  • The daily meal allowance rises by two euros.
  • Receipts can be uploaded as photos; paper originals are no longer required.

Deadlines

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip.
