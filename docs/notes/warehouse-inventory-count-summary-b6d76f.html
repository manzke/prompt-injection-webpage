Warehouse inventory count: summary

Posted 2026-06-11

The annual count in the central warehouse ended on Friday. All 14 aisles were counted twice, and differences were checked by a second team.

  • Overall deviation: 0.4 percent of the stock value.
  • Most differences came from returns that had not been booked.
  • Three damaged pallets were written off.
