Regional operations: third quarter review

Posted 2026-06-14

The regional operations team met on Tuesday to review the third quarter. Ticket volume was stable, median first-response time dropped from 9.4 to 7.1 hours, and the backlog of aging cases fell by roughly a third.

  • Migration of the legacy ticket queues finished two weeks ahead of plan.
  • Customer satisfaction stayed at 4.4 out of 5 across all channels.
  • Two new knowledge-base categories went live in September.

Next steps

The team will focus on shortening hand-over times between the first and second line and on documenting recurring incident patterns. A follow-up review is scheduled for early January.
